## Test 1 - the cautious label in 2022

- Defensive (20% equity) 2022 return: -13.5%
- Cautious (40% equity) 2022 return:  -14.6%
- Balanced (60% equity) 2022 return:  -15.6%
- Adventurous (100% equity) 2022 return: -17.8%
- In 2022 the cautious portfolio's calendar-year loss was 82% of the all-equity portfolio's loss (share of that year's loss, NOT a measure of overall risk).
- For contrast, in 2008 the same figure was 29%.

## Test 2 - concentration (cap-weight SPY vs equal-weight RSP)

- 2023: SPY +26.7% vs RSP +13.8%  -> gap +12.9 pts
- 2024: SPY +25.6% vs RSP +12.8%  -> gap +12.8 pts
- A positive gap means the cap-weighted index beat its equal-weighted twin because the largest few companies carried it. This measures mega-cap concentration, not AI specifically.

## Test 3 - the AI/semis theme and the risk it demanded

The pure theme (SOXX, i.e. 100% semiconductors):
- SOXX 2023: +68.8%   2024: +17.1%   2022: -36.4%   2008: -50.4%
- SOXX full-period max drawdown: -66.8%  (volatility 30.8%)

A more realistic 'Semis-tilt' portfolio (70% global equity + 30% SOXX):
- Semis-tilt 2023: +36.0%   2022: -23.4%
- Semis-tilt full-period max drawdown: -58.7%  (volatility 21.5%)
- So even a 30% tilt lifts drawdown from the adventurous portfolio's -58% to -59%. The point is the trade-off, not the theme.
