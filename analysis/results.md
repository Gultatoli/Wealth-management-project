## Test 1 - the cautious label in 2022

- Defensive (20% equity) 2022 return: -13.7%
- Cautious (40% equity) 2022 return:  -14.9%
- Balanced (60% equity) 2022 return:  -16.2%
- Adventurous (100% equity) 2022 return: -18.6%
- The cautious portfolio captured 80% of the all-equity loss despite holding only 40% equity.

## Test 2 - concentration (cap-weight SPY vs equal-weight RSP)

- 2023 return gap (SPY minus RSP): +12.9 pts
- 2024 return gap (SPY minus RSP): +12.8 pts
- A positive gap means the cap-weighted index beat its equal-weighted twin purely because a few mega-caps carried it: the concentration effect.

## Test 3 - the AI/semis theme and the risk it demanded

- Semis (SOXX) 2023: +68.8%   2024: +17.1%
- Semis (SOXX) 2022: -36.4%   2008: -50.4%
- Semis full-period max drawdown: -66.8%  (volatility 30.8%)
- The reward was real, but only a client who could hold through a -67% drawdown could ever collect it.
