What gets scored

  • OutcomeWhether the money does the job. The pot she has at the end, and the odds her income lasts to 95.
  • SuitabilityWhether what she held was ever defensible for her age, horizon and income need. Measured on the portfolio's real weights, not on its label.
  • ConductHow she was treated. Whether you explained, recorded, and stayed straight with her when it was uncomfortable.
